Insights

Where the stock is, and what needs attention

📍 Stock by Location
A ranked bar view of quantity on hand per internal location. Click any bar to open the quants behind it.
🔁 Top Products to Reorder
The six products with the highest quantity to order, one click through to the product record.
📊 7-Day Received vs Delivered Trend
A dual-series bar chart comparing incoming receipts against outgoing deliveries over the last week, rendered with Chart.js when available and a lightweight CSS fallback otherwise.
Setup

Up and running in two minutes

1
Install the module
Install Inventory Dashboard from Apps — it depends only on the standard stock app.
2
Open the dashboard
Find it as the first entry inside the Inventory app menu. It loads live counts on open and refreshes automatically every 30 seconds.
3
Pick a period, click a card
Switch between Today / Week / Month / All, then click any KPI, location, or reorder row to drill straight into the filtered list.
