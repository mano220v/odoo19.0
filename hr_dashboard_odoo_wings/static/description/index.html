Overview

Your workforce at a glance — always up to date

The HR Attendance Dashboard gives HR managers and administrators a real-time snapshot of the entire workforce. Install it and instantly see a live count of who is Present, On Leave, or Absent — for the full company and broken down by department. Every number is clickable and opens the matching filtered list for immediate action.

Features

Everything included — nothing to configure

◕ Live Summary Cards
Four large cards at the top of the dashboard: Total Employees, Present Today, On Leave Today, and Absent Today. Each card shows the count, the percentage of the total, and a colour-coded left border. Click any card to open a filtered list view instantly.
▮ Attendance Overview & Quick Summary
A two-panel row beneath the cards. Left panel shows animated horizontal progress bars (green · amber · red) with exact counts. Right panel shows three percentage circles (Present · On Leave · Absent) — all clickable to open the matching employee list.
🏢 Department-wise Attendance
A horizontally scrollable row of department cards — one per department, automatically generated from your Odoo data. Each card shows: department name, employee count, and three clickable circles — ● Green for Present, ● Amber for On Leave, ● Red for Absent — plus a stacked colour-coded mini bar at the bottom of each card. Departments sorted alphabetically. Click any circle to open that filtered employee list.
🔎 Click-to-Drilldown
Every metric is actionable. Clicking "Present Today" opens hr.attendance filtered to today's check-ins — showing employee, department, check-in, check-out, and worked hours. Clicking "On Leave" or "Absent" opens hr.employee filtered to those employee IDs — showing name, department, job position, and phone. Department circles also drilldown to the matching subset.
↻ Auto-Refresh
The dashboard silently refreshes every 5 minutes in the background using an OWL setInterval timer. The timestamp of the last refresh is shown in the footer. A manual Refresh button is also available in the top header for immediate updates.
Excel Export

Download a formatted attendance register in one click

The Export button sits in the header next to Refresh. Click it to open the Export modal. Choose Current Date for today's snapshot, or Other Date(s) to pick any date range up to 366 days. The server generates a styled .xlsx file and the browser downloads it automatically.

1. Click Export → 2. Choose Date Mode → 3. (Optional) Pick Range → 4. .xlsx Downloads
📅 Current Date
Downloads today's full attendance register in a single click — no date entry required. The filename is attendance_YYYYMMDD.xlsx.
ⓘ Best for: Daily HR morning check, shift supervisors, attendance managers
📅 Other Date(s) — Range
Pick any From and To date (up to 366 days). One row per employee per day. Filename: attendance_YYYYMMDD_to_YYYYMMDD.xlsx.
ⓘ Best for: Monthly payroll preparation, HR audits, leave reconciliation

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does this work for both Community and Enterprise?
Yes. The module depends only on hr, hr_attendance, and hr_holidays — all present in both editions of Odoo 19.
How is "Present" calculated?
An employee is counted as Present if they have at least one hr.attendance check-in record with a check_in timestamp falling within today's local calendar day (based on the logged-in user's timezone). Present always takes priority — if an employee has both an attendance record and an approved leave, they are counted as Present.
How is "On Leave" calculated?
An employee is counted as On Leave if they have a validated hr.leave record whose date range overlaps with today, and they are NOT already counted as Present.
How often does the dashboard update?
Automatically every 5 minutes. The footer shows the exact time of the last refresh. Use the Refresh button in the header for an immediate update.
What does the Excel export contain?
Sl No, Employee Name, Department, Shift Name, Date (range mode), Check-In Time, Check-Out Time, Present (Yes/No), Absent (Yes/No), On Leave (Yes/No). Colour-coded cells: green for Present, red for Absent, amber for On Leave. Includes company name, period, and generation timestamp in the header.
Is xlsxwriter required?
Yes, for the Excel export feature. xlsxwriter is a standard Python package included with most Odoo 19 installations. If not present, run pip install xlsxwriter on the server.
Can I click on a department circle to see those employees?
Yes. Every circle in the department row is clickable. Clicking the green Present circle in "Machine Shop" will open hr.attendance filtered to Machine Shop employees with a check-in today. The amber and red circles open hr.employee filtered to that department's on-leave or absent employees respectively.
