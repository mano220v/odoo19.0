📋 Field Tracker History Management

Complete audit trail for Purchase, Sales & Inventory —
who changed what, when, and from which value to which.

💰 Only Free

🎯 What Does This Module Do?

Every time an employee changes a Status, Price, Quantity or Discount on a Purchase Order, Sales Order, or Stock Transfer — the module records it automatically. No setup. No configuration. Just install and it works.

📌 Example: Arjun Sharma changed the Unit Price of "Dell Laptop 14in" on PO/2025/0042 from $1,200.00 → $1,050.00 on 25 May 2025 at 11:15 AM.

📦 Purchase Orders — Change History Tab

See every status, price, and quantity change directly inside the Purchase Order form, in the Change History notebook tab. Colour-coded by type for instant clarity.

Purchase Order form — Change History tab showing status, price and quantity changes with employee names

🛒 Sales Orders — Full Audit Including Discounts

Sales Orders also track Discount % changes in addition to status, price and quantity — giving you a complete picture of how deals evolved.

Sales Order form — tracking Status, Price, Quantity and Discount changes per line

🏭 Inventory Transfers — Status & Quantity Tracking

Stock Pickings record both Demand Quantity and Done Quantity changes on every move line, alongside the picking status transitions.

Stock Picking form — Change History for inventory status and quantity adjustments

🗂️ Global Change History Menu

In addition to the notebook tab on each document, a dedicated Change History menu is added under Purchase, Sales and Inventory apps — with search, filters, and group-by support.

Global Change History list — searchable, filterable view across all documents and employees

🚀 How to Use

Install the module — no configuration required.
Open any Purchase Order, Sales Order or Inventory Transfer.
Click the Change History tab in the notebook.
See the complete, colour-coded log of all changes made by your team.
Use the Change History menu (under Purchase / Sales / Inventory) for a global view.

⚙️ Technical Details
