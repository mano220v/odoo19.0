Sale Order Confirmed Stamp

Add a compact green seal to confirmed sale orders with your company name around the circle and the confirmation date inside.

The stamp appears automatically in the top-right corner once a quotation is confirmed.

Professional sale confirmation seal

This module adds a small stamp to the top-right area of the Sale Order form after confirmation. The stamp uses the order company name on the circular seal, shows SALE CONFIRMED in the center, and displays the confirmed date below.

SEAL
Company Name Around Circle
The company name appears once on the top arc, between the thick outer border and dashed inner line.
DATE
Confirmed Date Inside
The actual confirmation date is stored and shown inside the stamp for quick review.
AUTO
No Configuration
Install the module and confirm a quotation. The stamp appears automatically on confirmed orders.

What appears on the stamp

Top arc Company name
Center SALE CONFIRMED
Bottom inside Confirmed date
